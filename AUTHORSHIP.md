## Contributor identities in the history

This project's git history contains commits under more than one author
identity. The v0.1.0 and v0.2.0 implementation (2026-06-21) was committed
under the identity `Manus <manus@manus.im>`, which is also the name that
appears in the `LICENSE` copyright notice. Later commits — including the
`AUTHORSHIP.md` file itself, the v0.2.0 merge and the v0.3.0 cryptographic
hardening — are committed under the `Rishidar-lab` account.

Whether those identities belong to the same party is a question of fact
that the repository itself cannot settle, so it is not asserted here. The
`LICENSE` notice is left exactly as its original author wrote it, and the
full forensic record — including what the history does and does not prove —
is in [docs/PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md).

Anyone who believes they hold rights in part of this codebase, and who
believes those rights are not recorded, is encouraged to open an issue so
the discrepancy can be corrected.

## AI assistance

AI assistants, including Anthropic Claude, are used as development tools for
research, coding, review, testing, documentation, and iteration. Project
goals, architecture, product direction, prompts, acceptance or rejection of
generated output, and final responsibility remain with Rishidar-lab.

The repository contains no per-commit AI attribution, so the extent of AI
contribution to any individual file or commit is not recorded. This
disclosure is provided for transparency. It does not claim authorship of
third-party libraries, frameworks, datasets, APIs, templates, or other
dependencies; those retain their respective authorship and licenses.
